FAQ

What happens after 7 days?

Nothing is charged: no card is required. Choose a plan to continue; otherwise your account becomes read-only and your quotes stay available.

Are the AI prices reliable?

The AI uses French market prices and your description. It's a starting point: edit any line and set your own hourly rate.

Are my quotes compliant?

Yes: number, date, validity, company identity (SIRET, RCS/RM, VAT), insurance, itemised work, totals excl./incl. VAT, payment terms and a sign-off box.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in one click from your account. Your plan stops at the end of the paid period.

Where is my data stored?

In a secure database in Europe. Your data and your clients' data are never sold or used for anything else.
